# Otterfocus — Design Direction

## Three Possible Directions

### 1. Quiet Terrain
**Very Brief Intro:** A sun-washed photographic journal rooted in the stillness of the Mojave. It makes the work feel collected over time rather than displayed all at once.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Midnight Gallery
**Very Brief Intro:** A dark, gallery-like presentation with luminous images and restrained brass details. It would feel intimate and cinematic, but risks pushing the work toward an urban rather than natural identity.

**Probability:** 0.03

### 3. Field Notes
**Very Brief Intro:** An art-book approach inspired by naturalist records, hand-torn paper, and restrained captions. It would foreground observation and craft, with a quietly personal pace.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Selected Direction — Quiet Terrain

### Design Movement
**Contemporary editorial photography monograph**, informed by the quiet monumentality of Southwestern landscapes, refined independent magazines, and the unhurried sequencing of a printed portfolio book.

### Core Principles
1. **Image-led pacing:** Large photographs command each passage, with copy held in deliberate, breathable intervals.
2. **Asymmetrical poise:** Alignments shift intentionally between a narrow editorial text column and expansive visual fields, avoiding generic centered landing-page blocks.
3. **Material restraint:** Color, borders, and motion remain subdued so the photography is the principal subject.
4. **Honest specificity:** Language should clearly name Armand Arnau, Otterfocus, Las Vegas, and the services offered without keyword-stuffing or overclaiming.

### Color Philosophy
The visual field begins with **bone** (#F5F1EA), a warm paper-like neutral that gives images a gallery wall instead of a screen. **Charcoal** (#221F1D) provides calm, readable contrast. **Burnt clay** (#B5654A) is the ownable signature; it evokes sun-warmed canyon stone and marks moments of action without looking celebratory or ornamental. **Dusk mauve** (#6E5A6B) is used only as a recessive tonal accent, recalling a desert sky after sunset.

### Layout Paradigm
The website follows a **moving portfolio folio** rather than a conventional grid. Sections alternate between edge-to-edge photographs and offset editorial notes. A slim vertical “field index” may surface on larger screens, while each gallery behaves as a visual sequence with a chronological, page-turning cadence. Mobile retains the ordering and spaciousness, but resolves the asymmetric placements into a single graceful reading column.

### Signature Elements
1. **Topographic contour line:** A fine, imperfect contour motif that appears as a divider, footer detail, and menu accent.
2. **Numbered image folios:** Small image counts and location-style captions that make gallery pages feel like a printed edition.
3. **Scripture as a marginal note:** References appear in restrained uppercase with airy tracking and an adjacent rule—not as banner copy.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel tactile and measured. Links and controls respond with fine underline sweeps, slight image shifts, and confident contrast changes. Gallery navigation prioritizes clarity; inquiry actions stay visibly available without competing with the imagery. No interaction should resemble a dashboard or a generic wedding template.

### Animation
At first view, image and copy entrances use a short opacity-and-translate reveal (roughly 500–700ms, staggered by 60–90ms). Images can scale from 1.03 to 1.00 as they settle. Hover states use 180–240ms transform/opacity transitions only. There are no carousels, bouncing effects, parallax sweeps, or continuous decorative motion. All non-essential motion is suppressed for users who request reduced motion.

### Typography System
**Fraunces** carries display type: high-contrast but earthy, used in large, elegant sentence case for page titles and short declarative statements. **Work Sans** carries navigation, captions, labels, metadata, and body copy for clean legibility. Headlines should feel composed rather than ornate; overlines use Work Sans uppercase, 0.14–0.22em tracking, and modest size. Body copy stays at comfortable editorial measures, never wide or dense.

### Brand Essence
**Otterfocus is a Las Vegas photographer’s considered record of God’s creation, meaningful gatherings, and moments worth returning to.**

**Personality:** Grounded, observant, reverent.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are spare, specific, and contemplative. CTAs invite a next step with warmth and confidence, while microcopy explains rather than sells. Avoid filler, urgency, luxury clichés, and generic welcome language.

> “For the moments that become part of the landscape of your life.”

> “Tell me what you’re gathering around.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark should feel like a contemporary gallery imprint: wide-set small caps with a subtly altered crossbar in the **F** that suggests a horizon line. The graphic mark is a bold, text-free **otter silhouette viewed at water level**, resolved into a simple charcoal-and-clay stamp with a small aperture/circle cutout. It should work at favicon scale and as a recognisable seal beside the wordmark.

### Signature Brand Color
**Canyon Clay — #B5654A.**

## Style Decisions

- All hero and image-overlay copy must sit over an intentional dark gradient to maintain contrast, as the visual field changes between photograph selections.
- Photography will be represented by carefully distinct editorial placeholders until Armand’s original, licensed work can be migrated; final launch requires replacing every placeholder with supplied portfolio images.
- The print shop is presented as a refined catalogue/interest page until checkout, pricing, fulfilment, and platform decisions are confirmed.

- Every service or collection page opens with, or moves quickly into, a dominant photographic folio. Numbered labels always introduce or caption images rather than floating as decoration.
- Dusk Mauve is an atmospheric trace only; Bone is the principal field, Charcoal creates depth, and Canyon Clay remains the action color for CTAs, rules, and key numerals.
- Public-facing page copy reads as a complete Otterfocus monograph. Operational needs, asset migration, and integration requirements are handled outside the visitor experience.
